Karuppu Kavuni Kanji Mix

Rooted in tradition, made for today

Two ingredients. Black kavuni rice — the rice Chettinad families kept for people who needed strength — and green gram. Nothing else goes in, which is why the list is this short.

How to prepare
  1. Add water, cook, and enjoy.
  2. Mix 2 tbsp (30g) into 150ml water without lumps.
  3. Cook 5–7 minutes on a medium flame.
  4. Sweeten with palm jaggery or add milk to taste.
