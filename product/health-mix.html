How to prepare
  1. Mix 2 tbsp (30g) of powder into 100ml water, without lumps.
  2. Cook 5–7 minutes on a low flame.
  3. Add milk if you like it richer.
  4. Sweeten with palm jaggery or country sugar. Serve hot.
