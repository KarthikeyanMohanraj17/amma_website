Before you ask

The things people message us about.

How long does it keep?

Three months from the day you open the packet. That is short on purpose — nothing goes in to make it keep longer. Unopened and stored cool and dry, go by the date printed on your pack.

How should I store it?

Cool and dry, out of sunlight. Use a clean, dry spoon every time — a wet spoon is the fastest way to spoil a mix with no preservatives in it — and press the pack closed after each use.

Why is there no price on the site?

We are still settling the price list. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you the price for the sizes you want, the same day.

How do I pay?

On WhatsApp, after we confirm what you want. There is no checkout on this site and nothing is charged here.

Do you deliver outside Tamil Nadu?

Ask us. We arrange delivery personally for each order, so it depends on where you are and how much you want. We would rather tell you honestly than print a map we cannot keep to.

Is it safe for children, or during pregnancy?

Every ingredient is listed in full on this site and on the pack, so you can check it against anything you need to avoid. Several mixes contain nuts. For anything medical, please ask your doctor — we are cooks, not clinicians.
